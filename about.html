About NWONA

Neighbors working together to make Northwest Olympia a better place to live.

Our neighborhood

NWONA is a City of Olympia recognized neighborhood association. Its boundaries generally follow Budd Inlet to the east, Harrison Avenue to the south and Division Street to the west. The north boundary follows the Schneider Creek area, with specific inclusions and exclusions described by the association.

For the full boundary description see the original association information and the bylaws.

What we do

Represent neighbors

Communicate community concerns to the City of Olympia, including development, traffic, zoning and neighborhood services.

Bring people together

Organize neighborhood meetings, events, cleanups and opportunities to volunteer.

Care for public spaces

Promote parks, open space and safer routes for pedestrians and cyclists.

Share information

Keep residents informed of city notifications, community projects and local opportunities.

Selected community accomplishments

  • Helped establish Northwest Volunteer Park at Madison and Thomas.
  • Developed the Woodard Pathway connecting Woodard Avenue to West Bay Drive.
  • Supported protection of the Westside Heronry near Dickinson Avenue.
  • Worked with neighbors to advocate for crosswalk improvements on Harrison Avenue.

Our commitment to inclusion

NWONA welcomes neighbors and does not discriminate based on protected characteristics. The association's original nondiscrimination statement provides the full policy wording.

Neighborhood project highlights

  • West Bay Art Crossing: The heron and salmon sculptures, Guardians: Fighter and Watcher, were dedicated in 2021.
  • West Bay Woods: The Hays Avenue Trail connects with the Dickinson Trail through the wooded area.
  • Northwest Volunteer Park: An enduring example of local neighborhood stewardship.
  • Garfield Nature Trail Overlook: Developed by volunteers with support from Olympia's neighborhood grant program.

Historical event timeline (2005–2026)

2026

Summer Fun Fest at Woodruff Park; Fall Fest at Northwest Volunteer Park; monthly Northwest Volunteer Park cleanups.

2021

West Bay Art Crossing sculptures “Guardians: Fighter and Watcher” were dedicated.

2011

Transportation forum, Garfield and Thomas intersection-painting celebration, and Garfield Elementary remodeling community meeting.

2010

Annual gathering at Mariah Arts Center and a native plant restoration workshop.

2009

Olympia neighborhood history lecture.

2008

Annual meeting, West Bay Park groundbreaking, plant exchange, and neighborhood board meetings.

2007

Neighborhood seminars on websites, water resources, trees, land use, safety and historic homes.

2006

Community picnic, Harrison Avenue street-tree planting, plant swap and midwinter dance.

2005

Garfield Nature Trail overlook dedication, land-use game meeting and Building Better Neighborhoods town hall.

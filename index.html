Our association

Good neighbors make a great neighborhood.

The Northwest Olympia Neighborhood Association (NWONA) is a volunteer-led community group. We help residents stay informed about City of Olympia activities, organize occasional neighborhood events, and provide a place to raise local concerns.

We welcome neighbors who want to participate, volunteer, or simply keep informed.

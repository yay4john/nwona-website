Membership & giving

Get involved and support a neighbor-led association.

Become a member

NWONA is supported by member donations, volunteer time and occasional neighborhood grants. Membership is available to eligible adults who live in, own property in or own a business within the association boundaries.

Annual dues: $5–$25

Choose the amount that works for you. The association's published bylaws also allow four hours of in-kind volunteer service in place of dues.

Pay by check:
Northwest Olympia Neighborhood Association
1704 Bowman Ave NW
Olympia, WA 98502

Make the check payable to NWONA and write “NWONA contribution” on the memo line.

Pay online using the existing PayPal button →

NWONA states that it is not a 501(c) nonprofit and that contributions are not tax deductible. Please verify current payment instructions before sending money.

Volunteer

We welcome help with neighborhood events, park stewardship, communications and community projects. Email the board to get involved.
